# Guided public practices — 22 November 2026

Six existing URLs, bookmarks and root progress IDs are retained. No new paid-course content is exposed. Instructions and everyday examples are original adaptations, not quotations from books or participant testimonials.

The course-platform `server/practice-v2/methods.js` and `catalog.js` were reviewed for the progression from a lived scene, through an inner experiment, to reflection. The public exercises are standalone, with different wording and examples. They do not reproduce the personalized course journey.

## Concept mapping and primary references

- `pauza-smotritelya`: observer position and noticing an automatic role. `karta-kryuchkov`: recognizing the hook, non-participation or a non-escalating response, then returning attention. Reference: [Клип-трансерфинг, official excerpt](https://zelands.ru/klip-transerfing-chitat-onlajn-besplatno/).
- `plan-b`: distinguish the goal from inflated significance of a single outcome; a realistic alternative. Reference: [Важность](https://zelands.ru/vazhnost-v-transerfinge-realnosti/).
- `fakty-interpretatsii`: coordination of intention after an ordinary setback; keep possible continuations open and obtain facts. Reference: [Вершитель реальности, official excerpt](https://zelands.ru/vershitel-realnosti-chitat-onlajn-besplatno/).
- `svoya-tsel`: own interest, agreement of soul and reason, distinguish a goal from the route. References: the same excerpt and [Проектор отдельной реальности, official excerpt](https://zelands.ru/proektor-otdelnoj-realnosti-chitat-onlajn-besplatno/).
- `odin-realnyi-shag`: participate inside a target slide rather than only watch oneself; connect readiness to an actual attempt. References: [Ваши правила](https://zelands.ru/vashi-pravila/) and [Намерение](https://zelands.ru/namerenie-v-transerfinge-realnosti/).

The concept descriptions are short paraphrases. The detailed scenes, prompts and practical observation criteria are original editorial work. Metaphysical explanations are not presented as established causal mechanisms or promises of an external result. Coordination does not require denying a refusal or reframing a serious loss. The slide does not prescribe another person's response. All six exercises ask what the reader actually noticed, including uncertainty or no change.

## Experience and compatibility

- Dark reading surface throughout; no automatic light switch or theme toggle.
- No practice duration, countdown, timed shortcut, per-step checkbox or progress meter.
- Continuous guided experience: recognizable scene, concept and purpose, three passages, optional example, reflection, return cue.
- One optional attempt mark plus a private note. Marking an attempt also saves current note text through the existing account API.
- Historical step rows remain in the account database. The public account summarizes them as a previous attempt; it does not reinterpret them as newly completed sections.
- Stable root keys preserve notes and completion from the previous version. Guest saves still require login; no browser-local notes are introduced.
- Existing consent-dependent open, reading and completion telemetry remains; personal note contents are not added to event payloads.
